Finding us

Step inside MGH. We're right there.

01
Head to 55 Fruit Street

Navigate to Massachusetts General Hospital, 55 Fruit St, Boston, MA 02114. On-site garage parking is available.

→
02
Enter the main building

Use the main entrance on Fruit Street. You'll walk through the main lobby — no badge or check-in needed for the cafeteria.

→
03
Take the elevator to Floor 1

Head to the elevator bank and go to Floor 1 (ground level cafeteria floor). Follow the signs once you step off.

→
04
Follow signs to Eat Street

Look for the Eat Street Cafe signage. You'll see the Chef's Stage, salad bar, pizza counter, and Grab & Go straight ahead.
